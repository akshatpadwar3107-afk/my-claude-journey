1 "The listing says 'Junior' but asks for 3-5 years of experience — what level is this role actually banded at, and how does compensation reflect that?"
2 "Can you share the salary range for this position, and how it compares to mid-level roles with similar responsibilities?"
3 "You mention 'flexible remote with occasional in-office days' — how many days per month, and is relocation or local residency a hard requirement?"
4 "What does a typical week look like during a 'launch' period, and how is overtime or weekend work tracked or compensated?"
5 "With responsibilities spanning frontend, backend, DevOps, and QA, how is a new hire's workload prioritized in the first 90 days, and who decides what 'other duties as assigned' includes?"

Key Learnings:
* "Junior" + 3-5 yrs required, or expert-level demands across frontend/backend/DevOps/QA, signals underpaid overwork.
* Buzzwords like "rockstar," "hustle," "fast-paced" and perks with a catch (unlimited PTO, "use sparingly") hint at burnout culture.
* "Remote" tied to office days or commuting distance isn't really remote; no salary range or unpaid take-home tests are hiring red flags.
* Always verify with direct interview questions on level, pay, and workload rather than trusting the posting as-is.
