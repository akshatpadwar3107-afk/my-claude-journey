Key Learnings:
A strong single-file portfolio can look premium without a framework by combining Tailwind CDN + semantic HTML + small vanilla JS enhancements. The biggest impact
comes from clear personal branding (hero + about), quantified projects/achievements, and UX polish (dark/light toggle, active nav, smooth scroll, animations,
mobile-first layout). Good SEO metadata and recruiter-friendly copy matter as much as visual design for discoverability and conversions.
