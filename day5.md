Generic Roadmap:
              * One-size-fits-all approach
              * Broad recommendations
              * Limited personalization
              * Less aligned with individual goals


Personalized Roadmap:
                   * Tailored to skill level and objectives
                   * Customized projects and milestones
                   * More practical and actionable
                   * Clearer path toward desired outcomes

Key learnings:
"The quality of AI responses depends not only on the prompt, but also on the context provided."

By applying Context Engineering, I learned that AI produces significantly better results when it understands:

The goal:
* The user's background
* Relevant constraints
* Specific requirements

Observation: A personalized roadmap generated with proper context was far more relevant, actionable, and aligned with the intended outcome than a generic roadmap.
